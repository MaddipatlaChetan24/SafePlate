How it works

The model drafts. The rules decide.

Most AI recipe apps make one call and trust the answer. SafePlate treats the LLM as a drafting step and puts a deterministic checker between it and you.

01

Draft

The LLM proposes recipes as typed, validated structured output, not free text.

02

Verify

Every ingredient is checked against 9 allergen groups, your diet and your time limit.

03

Correct

Named violations go back to the model, which fixes only the recipes that failed.

04

Deliver

Anything still unsafe after two retries is dropped. You only ever see verified recipes.

What SafePlate tells the model when a recipe fails a check
Some recipes break my constraints:
- 'Paneer Butter Masala' contains 'cashews', which conflicts with allergy 'nuts'
- 'Hyderabadi Chicken Biryani' takes 75 min, over the 45 min limit
Replace or fix ONLY those recipes and return the full set again.
